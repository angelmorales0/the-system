# Privacy policy

The System is a personal app for one player. It is not sold or offered to the public.

## What it reads

With your permission, the app and its backend read your own data from these services:

- WHOOP: recovery, sleep, cycles, workouts, and body measurements.
- Strava: activities.
- Hevy: workouts and sets.
- Apple Health: workouts, nutrition, weight, and sleep, on the phone only.
- Screen Time: use of the apps you choose, on the phone only.

## Where it goes

- The backend runs on the player's own computer. It keeps access tokens in local files that are not shared.
- The phone keeps its own data on the phone.
- Summaries of the day go to the Claude API to plan the day's training, when a key is set.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
